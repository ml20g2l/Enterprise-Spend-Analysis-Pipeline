# Source provenance and reuse

## DEFRA monthly spending files

The 12 CSVs under `data/raw/defra/` are attributed to the UK Department for
Environment, Food & Rural Affairs (DEFRA). The official
[departmental spending collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/defra-departmental-spending-over-25000)
lists the March 2025 through February 2026 publications used here.

`reports/source_inventory.csv` records the controlled source month, official
publication page, repository path, byte size, SHA-256, encoding, row count,
schema, and observed date coverage for each file. Tests compare the preserved
files with these hashes. For cross-platform stability, the byte size and hash
normalise only Git checkout line endings to the publisher-style CRLF form; all
other bytes remain unchanged. This verifies repository content preservation but
does not prove byte identity with every current remote download.

Unless a publication carries a contrary notice, GOV.UK content is available
under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/),
subject to its conditions and exceptions. Attribution for the included files:

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
> Licence v3.0. Source: Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs.

No endorsement by DEFRA or the UK Government is implied. Third-party material,
logos, and personal data are outside the OGL unless separately authorised.

## Frankfurter reference rates

The cached EUR/GBP and USD/GBP responses under `data/raw/fx_rates/` were obtained
from the [Frankfurter API](https://frankfurter.dev/). They are reference data,
not expense records. The cache fixes the exact observations used by the
deterministic scenario and allows offline reruns.

## Fictional corporate data

Corporate expenses, vendors, departments, contracts, approval events, and
satisfaction responses are generated by this repository. They represent no
real organisation and are always labelled
`record_origin = 'synthetic_fictional_company'` and `is_synthetic = true`.
