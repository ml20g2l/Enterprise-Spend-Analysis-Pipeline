# Business requirements for the next implementation phase

## Real DEFRA scope

Supported questions are monthly spending by transaction date, supplier/entity/category concentration, source-file reconciliation, and duplicate/data-quality sensitivity. The publication contains spending over £25,000 and does not represent all organisational expenditure. Contract-number presence can be described, but contract compliance cannot be concluded without an authoritative contract master.

## Synthetic scope

Currency conversion, confirmed contract compliance, maverick-spend simulation, approval cycle time, SLA breaches, and vendor satisfaction require separate fictional-company data. Results must be labelled synthetic and must not be described as DEFRA performance.

## Deferred KPIs

KPI definitions and targets are deferred until the fact grains and source separation are approved. This phase does not invent targets or operational metrics.
